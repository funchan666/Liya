# Liya local preview

Liya follows the “92-情绪社交” design. This is a local preview; no account or backend is connected. See `DESIGN_COVERAGE.md` for the 29-artboard source inventory and outstanding service-dependent work.

- The mood diary, unsaved diary entries, profile, labels, example follows and blocks, message drafts, local likes, private notes, report drafts, composer and local posts use on-device UserDefaults. They do not sync and may be lost if the app is removed or its data is cleared. Optional fields preserve compatibility with existing text-only draft records, but migration has not been run or tested.
- Photo and video attachments and the profile picture are copied into Application Support when selected. The system picker keeps the original library items untouched. Removed or abandoned copies are currently retained rather than automatically deleted; they can consume storage. Files are not uploaded.
- The 40 supplied photos appear as clearly labelled illustrative posts with consistent local example profiles. The 10 supplied videos have no assigned author. Follows, blocks, likes and notes on examples are local actions and do not represent real people or social activity. Reports are unsent drafts.
- Local posts are explicitly labelled and appear in the square and profile, with edit, delete and media preview. They are not published online. The video feed uses one AVPlayer; a separate player is used only for a private attachment preview while the feed tab is inactive. Playback, seeking and thumbnail generation have not been runtime verified.
- Online authentication, public posts and uploads, messaging, moderation, remote reports and blocks, AI replies, purchases, account deletion, terms and privacy links are not connected. Local preview actions are not production services. Effective UGC moderation and a support contact are required before public release.
- `example.Liya` is a temporary development bundle identifier. The App Icon catalog uses the supplied opaque artwork resized to 1024×1024; signing, the final bundle identifier, product IDs and commerce behavior need final configuration.
- Original media remains in `ProjectMaterials`; only explicit copies in `Liya/Resources/Examples` and selected image sets enter the target. The examples total about 55.2 MiB before packaging; this is not an IPA or installed-size measurement. Some supplied art contains embedded English labels.
- The source uses iOS 27.1 SDK symbols and needs an Xcode SDK that declares them, with runtime availability protection for older iOS. Key custom controls use container margins and active division/occlusion regions, with scrollable controls when space is narrow. This is a source-level adaptation: an obstruction that leaves no usable 44-point region inside a fixed-height control currently falls back to its original bounds. Posture, keyboard, navigation, media import, right-to-left layout and resizing still need device validation.
- No build, tests, simulator, device, actual playback, purchase, service verification, or visual comparison of the running app was performed. Static inspection is not proof of runtime behavior or design fidelity.
