## App Name

Liya Mood Diary and Postcards

## App Description

A photo can bring back the walk home. A line in your diary can bring back what was on your mind. In Liya, choose a mood for the day and write down the detail you want to remember. Open an entry from the calendar or look back over the past seven or thirty days to see which feelings came up most often.

Between days turns your diary into illustrated postcards. Choose one date, or pair two days and read the entries together. A card starts with the dates and moods. You decide whether to include a few words from your notes. Preview the card, then share the image with someone you choose.

Take a quiet minute with three prompts about what you can see, hear, and feel. Keep a passing thought in your private notebook, or save a memory with a caption, tags, and up to nine photos or videos. Leave it as a draft if you want to finish it later.

Explore the collection of photographs and short videos. Follow the profiles you enjoy, leave a like, or comment on a detail that caught your eye. Members who follow each other can exchange private messages, photos, and videos. You can report a comment or block a member from inside Liya.

Daybreak postcard paper is included. Spend coins on Tideglass paper, the Mood Prism diary palette, or the Profile Glow accent. Your welcome coins cover all three styles. Additional coin packs are optional.
