## AI conversations and third-party processing

When you choose to use Liya AI and agree to AI processing, Liya sends your message and a limited amount of your recent AI conversation to Groq through Supabase to generate a response. Supabase hosts the account and conversation data and the service that forwards requests. Groq processes the submitted conversation to provide the AI response. Your AI messages and responses are stored with your account so that you can continue the conversation. Liya AI is an automated companion, not a person or a professional support service. Please consider carefully before sharing sensitive information.

You may withdraw your AI processing consent in the AI privacy controls. After withdrawal, new AI requests will not be sent unless you agree again. Withdrawal does not erase conversations already saved in your account or recall requests already sent. Account deletion removes the Liya account's stored AI conversation and consent records as part of the deletion process. Processing and retention by Groq and Supabase are also subject to their respective privacy and service terms.

Provider information: [Groq privacy information](https://groq.com/privacy-policy/) and [Supabase privacy information](https://supabase.com/privacy).
