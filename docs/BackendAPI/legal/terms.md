# Liya Terms of Service

Effective 4 November 2026.

## Using Liya

Liya provides a space to explore emotional content, keep personal thoughts, and participate in a community. Some features may be unavailable while the service develops. Use the app lawfully and respect other people.

## Accounts and your content

You may use Liya with a guest account, an email and password, or Sign in with Apple. A guest account may be lost if its session is removed, the device is lost, or the app is removed. An email or Apple sign-in creates or accesses its own account and does not automatically combine it with a guest account. Keep your sign-in details secure. You are responsible for the content you choose to create and for using only material you have the right to use.

## Conduct and availability

Do not use the service for harassment, exploitation, unlawful content, impersonation, spam, or attempts to disrupt the service. Community posts and comments are visible to other members; messages are for their participants. You can block members or report content, though report review has not yet been configured. Features may change or become unavailable. Local drafts and device-only actions are not published or delivered to other people.

## AI companion

Liya AI is an automated service that can generate conversational responses. Its responses may be inaccurate or unsuitable and do not replace professional advice or emergency services. You decide whether to use it and should avoid sharing information you do not wish to send. Before AI conversations are sent to a third-party provider, the app explains the sharing and asks for your permission; you may withdraw that permission in the AI privacy controls. Our Privacy Policy explains how the feature handles information.

## Support

The proposed support address is support@liya.com. It has not yet been confirmed or activated.
