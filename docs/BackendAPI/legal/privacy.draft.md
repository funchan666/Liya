# Liya Privacy Policy

Draft for the planned online service — not yet in effect. 30 October 2026.

## Information you provide

When available, Liya may process account identifiers, profile details, posts, media, messages, reports and other information you choose to submit. Some mood or diary entries can be sensitive; consider what you choose to include. Features not connected in the current preview do not send that preview data to the online service.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide the requested features, maintain account sessions, deliver content, respond to reports and protect the service. Public posts and profile details may be visible to other users. Private items should be accessible only as needed for the relevant feature.

## Service providers and permissions

The planned online service uses Supabase for account, database and file hosting. A photo-library permission or picker may be used when you choose media; you can decline access. Further providers will be described when actually added. No AI conversation provider is currently connected.

## Your choices and security

You can choose what information to submit and, where available, edit or remove it using the controls provided. Security measures are intended to restrict access but cannot guarantee absolute security. Anonymous accounts may not be recoverable after signing out or losing the device unless a recovery method is added.

## Contact and updates

No support channel has been activated yet. The proposed address support@liya.com is not confirmed to be operational. This draft must be updated to match the actual service and published controls before it takes effect.
