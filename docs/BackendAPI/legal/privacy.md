# Liya Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026.

## Information handled

Liya uses an anonymous account identifier and session to connect to the service. The service stores the account's coin balance and welcome-gift record, account profile and selected profile photo, saved mood entries and private thoughts, community profile, posts and attachments, comments, likes, follows, blocks, messages and attachments, automated chat messages and responses, and reports you choose to submit. When the app connects, the hosting provider may process connection information such as IP address and ordinary service logs. Earlier device-only profiles and journal entries, local drafts and other features described in the app as device-only remain on your device unless you choose to send them.

## How information is used

The account and session support access to Liya, its example content, community and your saved account information. Your community name and introduction, and any avatar you choose to share, are visible to other signed-in members. Posts, attachments and comments are visible to the community; messages and their attachments are available to their participants. People you block cannot interact with you through the community while the block is in place. The automated chat selects a prewritten response based on words in your message; it is not a human or a professional support service. Supabase provides authentication, database and file hosting for the service. Reports are stored, but a review service has not yet been configured. When you choose to view a coin pack, Apple supplies its App Store product information. Purchases are not currently enabled.

## Photos and your choices

You can choose whether to select photos or videos using the system picker. Selected items may be copied into the app's local storage and uploaded when you share a post or send a message; large videos may be compressed before upload. You may request deletion of your account in the app. This removes its cloud profile, posts, conversations, media and coin balance; local drafts remain on your device. A pseudonymous deletion receipt is retained to resolve an interrupted request; receipts older than 30 days are eligible for cleanup when another deletion is processed. Removing the app or clearing its data can remove local drafts and may make an anonymous account inaccessible. Take care when entering sensitive mood information or sharing content.

## Security and contact

Access controls are used to protect account information, but no system can guarantee absolute security. If the service changes, this policy may be updated. The proposed support address is support@liya.com; it has not yet been confirmed or activated.
